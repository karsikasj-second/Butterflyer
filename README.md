# Butterflyer
A real-time generative hypersigil rhythm instrument in Python.

Butterflyer is a real-time generative rhythm instrument written in Python. At its center is a rotating hypersigil inspired by the Shri Yantra, whose continuously changing geometric relationships act as part of the sequencing and modulation system itself. Instead of relying on a conventional step sequencer, Butterflyer uses interacting phases, drift, symmetry, mutation and probability to generate evolving breakbeats, trip-hop, drum & bass and abstract electronic rhythms.

The instrument combines a graphical performance interface with real-time synthesized percussion, spectral modulation and controlled randomness. Its aim is not simply to produce random beats, but to behave like a small autonomous rhythmic organism whose previous states influence what happens next.

Butterflyer is designed as an experimental sound-art instrument: something to play, observe, perturb and allow to evolve.
